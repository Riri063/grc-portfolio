# GRC Portfolio

Risk register, control mapping, and gap analysis portfolio built for a GRC graduate scheme application. Covers ISO 27001 Annex A mapping, risk scoring, and treatment planning for a mock e-commerce business.

## Scenario

All work here is based on a fictional mid-sized e-commerce business, created to give the risk scoring and control mapping realistic, consistent context rather than abstract textbook examples.

## Contents

### Risk Register & Mitigation Strategy — `GRC_Risk_Portfolio.xlsx`
A 15-scenario risk register spanning Operational, Cyber, and Compliance risk categories. Includes:
- Qualitative Likelihood × Impact scoring for inherent risk
- Defined treatment plans (Treat / Tolerate / Transfer / Terminate) with assigned owners
- Residual risk recalculation post-treatment
- Supporting Dashboard and Heat Map views for portfolio-level reporting
- Documented scoring methodology and risk appetite assumptions

### Control Mapping & Gap Analysis — *in progress*
Mock business policies mapped against ISO 27001 Annex A controls, identifying compliance gaps (particularly in access management and third-party monitoring) and producing a prioritized remediation roadmap. Coming soon.

## Why this exists

Built as a hands-on complement to academic study in Applied Cybersecurity, focused specifically on Governance, Risk, and Compliance — to practice the kind of deliverables (risk registers, control mappings, gap analyses) that a GRC analyst role actually produces, rather than relying on theory alone.
